Campus Connect — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Campus Connect ("we", "the app") is a social networking application for students and communities. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Campus Connect mobile app and website.

Developer note: Replace the contact email below with your real support address before submitting to Google Play and the Apple App Store. Both stores require a working contact method.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data: email address and/or phone number, and a password or sign-in code used to create and access your account.
  • Profile data: display name, username, course/field, biography, profile photo, and gender. Contact email/phone are only shown to others if you explicitly choose to share them.
  • Content you create: posts, stories/statuses, text messages, voice messages, photos, and videos you upload.
  • Communication metadata: information needed to deliver chats, notifications, and to connect audio/video calls between users.

2. Camera and microphone

The app can request your camera and microphone only for features you start: taking a profile photo, recording a voice message, or joining a voice or video call. These permissions are not used in the background, and media is only sent to the people you choose to communicate with.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To show your posts, stories, and messages to the audience you select.
  • To enable chats, voice messages, and audio/video calls.
  • To send notifications about reactions, comments, follows, and messages.

4. Anonymity / display of identity

Campus Connect can display other members in a simplified, anonymous form (for example, showing a member as "Student" with a default avatar) so that people you interact with are less easily identifiable. Your own profile information is still stored so the app can function and so you can manage your account.

5. Where data is stored

Application data (accounts, profiles, messages, and uploaded media) is hosted by our backend database and storage provider, Supabase. Media for calls is transmitted peer-to-peer where possible using standard web real-time communication (WebRTC).

6. Data sharing and advertising

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not include third-party advertising or advertising trackers.
  • We only share data to provide the features described above or when required by law.

7. Data retention and deletion

You can edit your profile and delete the content you create within the app. If you want your account and associated data deleted, contact us and we will remove it in line with applicable law.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures such as encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

Campus Connect is intended for students and adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@campusconnect.app (replace with your real email).
