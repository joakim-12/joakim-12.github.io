University systems worldwide

Every university names its online tools differently. This page tells you which one is which.

What is the difference?
🎓 Student portal / ENT
The door to your own account: timetables, fees, results, letters. In French-speaking countries it is called the ENT (Espace Numérique de Travail); in the US simply the student portal.
📚 LMS / VLE
Where the courses live: slides, assignments, quizzes, submission. Called a learning management system (Moodle, Blackboard, Canvas, Brightspace, Teams) or a virtual learning environment.
🗂 Registry / Records
The university's own database of you: admission, enrolment, transcripts, graduation. Often run by the Registry office and sometimes a separate system from the portal.
🌐 Official website
The public site of the institution. Start here if you cannot find the portal, and never trust a login page you reached from a text message.
